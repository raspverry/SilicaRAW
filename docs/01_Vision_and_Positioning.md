# 01 — SilicaRAW Vision & Positioning

Status: GO

## Mission

Build a credible, beautiful, open-source RAW photo editor for macOS and Apple Silicon.

## One-line Definition

**SilicaRAW is an open-source RAW photo editor built for Apple Silicon.**

## Positioning

SilicaRAW is not an AI app with a photo UI. It is a photo editor first.

It combines:

- RAW photo editing
- macOS-first UX
- Metal-first rendering
- Local-first storage
- MLX-powered intelligent editing, only where useful
- Optional plugin/MCP automation, permissioned and logged

## Why It Should Exist

Current options leave a gap:

- Lightroom is powerful but subscription-based and heavy.
- Darktable/RawTherapee are powerful but often intimidating and not Apple-like.
- RapidRAW is promising but cross-platform and not deeply Apple Silicon/MLX positioned.
- Apple Photos is polished but not a full professional RAW editor.

SilicaRAW targets the gap: a modern Apple Silicon-first open-source RAW editor.

## Non-Negotiable Identity

```txt
Photo editor first.
AI second.
MCP third.
```

## Target Users

1. Mac photographers who want a local, non-subscription RAW editor.
2. Creators who need fast photo editing and export workflows.
3. Developers interested in Rust, Tauri, Metal, MLX, and open-source creative tools.
4. Reviewers/local guides who process food/cafe/travel photos.

## Strategic Differentiators

- Metal-first editor core
- Apple-like UI/UX
- Local-first and privacy-first
- Non-destructive editing
- MLX tools integrated into editing, not marketed as magic
- Extensible but safe plugin/MCP design

## Final Verdict

GO.

Proceed, but protect the identity: SilicaRAW must remain a serious RAW photo editor.
